// About

A rewards distribution system, in plain terms.

The Tycoon Club is a private members' programme built around the distribution of on-chain rewards. The mechanics are simple: members participate, rewards are calculated on a published schedule, and the Club distributes them in USDT.

  • 01
    Membership-based. The Club is closed. Members are admitted by enquiry and review, not by automatic sign-up. The record is shared with admitted members in full.
  • 02
    Distributed, not pooled. The Club does not hold member capital, operate a fund, or pool deposits for investment. Member entries are membership fees, not custody balances.
  • 03
    On-chain settlement. Every distribution settles in USDT on the Arbitrum network. Members can verify their payouts on-chain at any time using the addresses provided in the Member Pack.
// How It Works

Three steps to a distribution.

The full distribution schedule, terms and risk disclosures are provided to members in their Member Pack after admission. Public summary below.

// Step 01

Join the Club

Submit an enquiry. If the Club is a fit, the team reaches out for a private conversation. Admission is by review; the Club may decline any enquiry at its discretion.

// Step 02

Participate

Active members participate in the Club's activities. The full rules and reward structure are disclosed in the Member Pack provided after admission.

// Step 03

Receive Distribution

The Club calculates and distributes rewards on a published schedule. All distributions settle in USDT on Arbitrum and are verifiable on-chain.
