// Setting Expectations

What Tycoon Club is — and isn’t.

Transparency about scope is part of our standards. Read both columns before applying.

What it is

A private members community for skill-based competition.

  • A members community for skill-based gaming and digital entertainment.
  • An access platform for tournaments and leagues with published, transparent rules.
  • Contests with prize pools funded from defined entry fees and sponsorships.
  • Application-based with KYC, eligibility checks and jurisdictional review.
What it is not

Not a scheme, not a yield product, not MLM.

  • Not an investment, fund, securities offering, derivative or yield product.
  • Not a source of guaranteed, “passive” or “unlimited” income.
  • Not a multi-level marketing or pyramid structure paying you ongoing income from others’ activity.
  • Not available to residents of restricted jurisdictions or persons under 18.
// Member Experience

A focused, well-governed community.

Approved members get access to skill-based contests, community events and a structured rewards programme operated under published, audited rules.

01

Skill-Based Contests

Competitive events with published rules, fixed entry fees and prize pools defined in advance. Outcomes depend on skill — not on the activity of other members.

02

Tournaments & Leagues

Seasonal leagues with transparent leaderboards, audited results and clear eligibility criteria.

03

Member Events

Member-only AMAs and educational sessions with the operating team. Information — not solicitation.

04

KYC & Eligibility

Identity verification, sanctions screening and jurisdictional review before activation.

05

Transparent Reporting

Where contests use digital assets, settlement details are made available to participants.

06

Responsible Participation

Self-limits and cooling-off tools. Never spend more than you can afford to lose.

// Community Growth Programme

Referrals, done responsibly.

A one-time, capped community credit per KYC-verified referral — not a multi-tier compensation plan, not an ongoing share of others’ activity.

I

One-time credit

Fixed, capped credit per qualified referral. No recurring commissions, no tiers, no “alliances”.

II

No income claims

We do not present referral activity as a job, income stream or business opportunity.

III

Eligibility required

Referrals only qualify after the referred applicant completes KYC and jurisdictional review.
