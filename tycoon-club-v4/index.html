// About

What the Club is.

In short

The Tycoon Club is a private members' club. Members are admitted by enquiry, and share in what the Club produces.

A club, not a scheme. A membership, not an investment.
The longer read

The Club exists to bring together members who value quietness, consideration, and a long view. Membership is granted by enquiry only, after a private conversation between the Club and the prospective member.

From its own operating activity, the Club runs a reward programme for active members. Rewards are paid in USDT and settled on the Arbitrum network. The full record of what the Club has paid to date is published below.

The Club is not an investment fund. It does not solicit deposits, hold member capital, or promise returns. Members join with a one-time entry; any rewards that follow are a benefit of active membership, not a financial return.

01

By enquiry only.

There is no public sign-up. Every prospective member speaks with the Club directly before joining.

02

Open on the ledger.

All rewards to members settle on a public chain. The Club publishes its record openly and refreshes it regularly.

03

Private on the roster.

Members are never publicly named. The Club identifies its activity in aggregate only.

// How It Works

Three quiet steps.

i.

Enquire.

A prospective member registers an enquiry. The Club gets in touch directly for a private conversation about whether the Club is a good fit.

ii.

Join.

If both sides are satisfied, the member is admitted. The Club covers the practicals at this stage; nothing is decided unilaterally on either side.

iii.

Stay active.

Active members are eligible for the Club's reward programme. Members who go dormant are not. The Club is built for people who stay involved.

// Questions

Things people ask us.

Is the Tycoon Club an investment?

No. The Tycoon Club is a private members' club. Members are admitted by enquiry with a one-time entry. Any rewards that follow are a benefit of active membership, not a return on invested capital. The Club is not registered as an investment product and does not present itself as one.

How are member rewards funded?

Rewards are paid from the Club's own operating activity. They are not paid from a pool of new-member entries. All rewards settle in USDT on the Arbitrum network and can be verified on-chain.

Is the past record a guide to future results?

No. The figures shown describe rewards paid between November 2025 and February 2026. They are a record of past events. They are not a forecast or a promise for any future period. Individual results have varied and will continue to vary. A new member should expect that their experience may differ from any past figure shown.

Does the Club hold my money?

No. The Club does not take custody of member capital, operate a fund, or pool member contributions for investment. The one-time entry is a membership fee, not a deposit or investment.

How do I join?

Joining is by enquiry only. Prospective members register their interest, the Club reaches out for a private conversation, and admission is decided one-on-one. The Club is not open to automatic sign-up, and reserves the right to decline any enquiry without explanation.

Can I leave?

Yes. Members are free to step away at any time. Stepping away ends the membership and the entitlement to future rewards. The Club does not hold members in any minimum term.
