Compliance Notice.

Last updated: May 2026. Please read this notice in full before applying for membership or participating in any Tycoon Club contest.

This is a plain-language summary

The summary supplements, but does not replace, the full Terms of Service and Privacy Notice that applicants must accept before activation. Where the full terms and this summary conflict, the full terms prevail.

01Tycoon Club is not an investment.

Tycoon Club is a private members community offering access to skill-based competitive gaming and digital entertainment. Tycoon Club does not sell securities, investment contracts, derivatives, funds, yield products or tokenised financial instruments. No statement on this website or in our materials is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security or financial product.

02No guaranteed returns — ever.

We do not promise that any member will earn money, recover entry fees or generate any income through participation in Tycoon Club. Words such as “guaranteed”, “passive”, “unlimited”, “lifetime” or “risk-free” do not apply to Tycoon Club and should be treated as a red flag wherever you see them in this category.

03Risk Notice.

Participation in skill-based contests involves real risk. Entry fees are at risk and may not be returned. Where contests are settled in digital assets, the value of those assets can fluctuate significantly and may decline. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Only participate with funds you can afford to lose.

04Eligibility & KYC.

Membership is by application only. All applicants are subject to identity verification (KYC), sanctions and PEP screening, jurisdictional eligibility review and, where applicable, source-of-funds checks. We may decline any application at our sole discretion, with or without reason. Members must be at least 18 years old.

05Restricted Jurisdictions.

Tycoon Club is not available to residents or nationals of jurisdictions where our service is restricted by sanctions or local law. This presently includes (without limitation) the United States, China, North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba and any country subject to comprehensive sanctions. The restricted list may be updated at any time without notice.

06Terms Summary.

Approved members must accept the full Terms of Service before activation. The Terms govern, among other things: contest rules and prize-pool maths; fair play and anti-cheat obligations; suspension and termination; dispute resolution; and the limited circumstances in which a contest may be voided.

07Privacy.

We process personal data only as necessary to evaluate applications, operate the community, satisfy legal obligations and provide member services. Applicants may withdraw their request or request deletion of their data at any time by emailing compliance@tycoonclub.example, subject to mandatory retention obligations.

08Marketing & Communications.

We do not engage influencers, affiliates or third parties to make income claims about Tycoon Club. If you have seen Tycoon Club promoted with phrases such as “guaranteed returns”, “passive income” or “unlimited earnings”, that communication is unauthorised and inconsistent with this notice.

09Acknowledgement.

By submitting an application or using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Compliance Notice, that you understand the risks described above, and that Tycoon Club, its operating entity and its affiliates are not liable for losses arising from the risks set out herein.

This Compliance Notice does not constitute legal, financial or tax advice. Applicants are encouraged to consult independent counsel in their jurisdiction before participating.
