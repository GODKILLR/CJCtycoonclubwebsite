Operating Principles

Three commitments that shape every decision.

01

Compliance first

We build to the rules. If a feature can’t be operated transparently, it doesn’t ship.

02

Transparency by default

Every contest publishes its rules, entry fees and prize-pool maths in advance.

03

Member welfare

Self-limits, cooling-off and clear “you can lose your entry fee” messaging.
