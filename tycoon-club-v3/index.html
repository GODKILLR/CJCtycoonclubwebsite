About

What the Club is, in plain terms.

In short

The Tycoon Club is a private members' club. Members join, take their place, and share in what the Club distributes.

The earlier you join, the better your standing in the Club.
The longer read

Every member is admitted to a register. Your place in that register is set at the moment you join and stays yours for as long as your membership is active.

The Club runs a reward distribution programme funded from its own operating activity. Active members receive payouts in USDT, settled on the Arbitrum network. The full record of what has been distributed is published below.

The Club is not a fund and does not hold member capital. Members join with a one-time entry, and any rewards that follow are a benefit of being a member in good standing.

Principle 01
Your place is yours.

Once you are in the register, your position is fixed at the time of joining and held for as long as your membership is active.

Principle 02
The record is public.

All member payouts settle on a public ledger. The distribution record is published openly and refreshed regularly.

Principle 03
Members stay private.

Members are never publicly named. Top earners are shown by rank and amount only, never by name or wallet.

Principle 04
Active members earn.

Payouts go to members who stay active in the Club. Dormant memberships do not draw rewards.

Questions

Things people ask us.

Is the Tycoon Club an investment?

No. The Tycoon Club is a private members' club. Members join with a one-time entry to be added to the register. Any payouts that follow are a benefit of being an active member, not a return on invested capital. The Club is not registered as an investment product and does not present itself as one.

How are member payouts funded?

Payouts are funded from the Club's own operating activity. They are not funded from new-member entries pooled together. All payouts settle on the Arbitrum network and can be verified on-chain. Members can request the relevant wallet addresses for independent checking.

Are the past payouts a guarantee of future results?

No. The figures shown describe payouts between November 2025 and February 2026. They are a record of past events. They are not a forecast or a promise for any future period. Individual results have varied widely and will continue to vary. New members should expect that their results may differ from any past figure shown.

What happens if I stop being active?

Membership in good standing requires staying active in the Club. Long dormancy or failure to keep membership current can result in reduced payouts or suspension. Full terms are provided when a member joins.

Can I buy a higher position in the register?

No. Positions in the register are assigned in the order members join, and cannot be bought, sold, or moved out of sequence.

How do I join?

Joining is by enquiry and conversation. Prospective members register their interest, the Club gets in touch directly, and the right entry path is discussed one-on-one. The Club is not open to automatic sign-up, and reserves the right to decline an enquiry.
