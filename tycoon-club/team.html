Our operating principles

Three commitments that shape every decision — from product design to communications.

⚖

Compliance first

We build to the rules. If a feature can’t be operated transparently, it doesn’t ship. Restricted-jurisdiction access controls are in place at the front door.

🔍

Transparency by default

Every contest publishes its rules, entry fees and prize-pool maths in advance. Every payout is reconciled against those rules.

🛡

Member welfare

Self-limits, cooling-off and clear “you can lose your entry fee” messaging are non-negotiable parts of the experience.
