Setting Expectations

What Tycoon Club is — and isn’t

Transparency about scope is part of our membership standards. Please read both columns before applying.

Tycoon Club is

  • A private members community focused on skill-based competitive gaming and digital entertainment.
  • An access platform for tournaments, leagues and member-only events with published, transparent rules.
  • A community where prize pools for contests are funded from defined entry fees and sponsorships, distributed in line with published rules.
  • Application-based and subject to identity verification, eligibility checks and applicable law in your jurisdiction.

Tycoon Club is not

  • An investment scheme, securities offering, fund or yield product.
  • A source of guaranteed, “passive” or “unlimited” income.
  • A public marketplace — access is restricted to approved members only.
  • Available to residents of restricted jurisdictions or persons under the age of 18.

Common questions, answered honestly

If something isn’t clear, please ask before you apply. We’d rather decline a well-informed applicant than onboard a confused one.

How is this not an investment?

Membership grants access to a community and to skill-based contests with defined entry fees and prize pools. There is no expectation of profit derived from the efforts of others, and no token, share or contract that represents an investment in Tycoon Club.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Entry fees for contests are at risk, and digital assets can fluctuate in value. Only participate with funds you can afford to lose. See our Compliance Notice.

Where are you registered?

Operating entity and registration details are disclosed on the Compliance page. We restrict access to jurisdictions where our service is not authorised.

Why is access gated?

Because we need to confirm who you are, where you live and whether you understand what you’re joining. The form on the next page captures the minimum information required to evaluate your application.
