What Tycoon Club really is

Tycoon Club is a private members community for adults interested in skill-based competitive gaming and digital entertainment. Approved members get access to:

  • Tournaments and leagues with published rules, fixed entry fees and pre-defined prize pools.
  • Member-only events, AMAs and educational sessions with the operating team.
  • A community growth programme that pays a one-time, capped credit per eligible referral — not a recurring share of others’ activity.
  • Transparent reporting on contest outcomes and rewards distribution.

What it is not

  • Not an investment, fund, securities offering, yield product or token sale.
  • Not a multi-level marketing or pyramid structure. There are no “tiers” that pay you ongoing income based on the downstream activity of other members.
  • Not a source of guaranteed, “passive” or “unlimited” income. Outcomes in any contest depend on skill and luck and can result in losses.

Your next steps

  1. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for our information packet.
  2. If your petition is shortlisted, you will be invited to complete KYC.
  3. After successful KYC and jurisdictional review, you will receive a membership offer with the full terms attached.

Need to update your details or withdraw your petition? Email compliance@tycoonclub.example at any time. We will action data requests in line with our Privacy commitments.
