House Principles

Three commitments that shape every decision.

From product design through to communications.

I.

Compliance first

We build to the rules. If a feature can’t be operated transparently, it doesn’t ship. Restricted-jurisdiction controls are in place at the front door.

II.

Transparency by default

Every contest publishes its rules, entry fees and prize-pool maths in advance. Every payout is reconciled against those rules.

III.

Member welfare

Self-limits, cooling-off and clear “you can lose your entry fee” messaging are non-negotiable parts of the experience.
