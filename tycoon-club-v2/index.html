Public Notice

This is not an investment product.

Tycoon Club does not sell securities, offer investment contracts or yield products, guarantee profit, or promise “passive”, “lifetime” or “unlimited” income. Participation in any contest or community programme involves risk, including the loss of entry fees or digital assets. Please read the full Compliance Notice before petitioning for access.

I. Setting Expectations

What Tycoon Club is — and isn’t.

Transparency about scope is part of our standards. Both columns are part of the same house rule. Read them both before you petition.

— What it is

A house of skill-based competition.

  • A private members community for skill-based competitive gaming and digital entertainment.
  • An access platform for tournaments, leagues and member events with published, transparent rules.
  • Contests with prize pools funded from defined entry fees and sponsorships, distributed in line with published rules.
  • Application-based, subject to KYC, eligibility checks and applicable law in your jurisdiction.
— What it is not

Not a scheme. Not a yield product.

  • Not an investment, fund, securities offering, derivative or yield product.
  • Not a source of guaranteed, “passive” or “unlimited” income.
  • A public marketplace — access is restricted to approved members only.
  • Not available to residents of restricted jurisdictions or persons under the age of 18.
II. Member Experience

A focused, well-governed community.

Approved members get access to skill-based contests, member events and a structured rewards programme operated under published, audited rules.

I.

Skill-Based Contests

Competitive events with published rules, fixed entry fees and prize pools fully defined in advance. Outcomes depend on skill — not on the activity of other members.

II.

Tournaments & Leagues

Seasonal leagues with transparent leaderboards, audited results and clear eligibility criteria. Every payout is governed by the published rule set for that event.

III.

Member Events

Members-only meet-ups, AMAs and educational sessions with the operating team. Designed to inform — not to solicit investments or guarantee outcomes.

IV.

KYC & Eligibility

All members complete identity verification and a jurisdictional review before activation. Access is restricted where required by applicable law or sanctions programmes.

V.

Transparent Reporting

Where contests use digital assets, settlement details are made available to participants. Historical performance is not indicative of future results.

VI.

Responsible Participation

Self-limits, cooling-off tools and signposting to support services. Members are encouraged never to spend more than they can afford to lose.

III. Member Privileges

What membership unlocks.

Approved members receive access to a curated calendar of experiences and a private community of fellow members. Membership is granted at our discretion and is not transferable.

I.

Curated events

A seasonal programme of tournaments, live broadcasts, watch-parties and members-only gatherings — each scheduled and run by the operating team.

II.

Private community

Access to member-only channels for conversation with the operating team and other approved members. Civility and confidentiality are part of the member agreement.

III.

Members-only content

Briefings, interviews and behind-the-scenes material from the operating team. Editorial in nature — no investment or income guidance.

IV. Plain Questions

Asked & answered, honestly.

If something isn’t clear, ask before you apply. We’d rather decline a well-informed applicant than onboard a confused one.

— Q.01

How is this not an investment?

Membership grants access to a community and to skill-based contests with defined entry fees and prize pools. There is no expectation of profit derived from the efforts of others, and no token, share or contract that represents an investment in Tycoon Club.

— Q.02

Can I lose money?

Yes. Entry fees for contests are at risk, and digital assets can fluctuate in value. Only participate with funds you can afford to lose. See our Compliance Notice.

— Q.03

Where are you registered?

Operating-entity and registration details are disclosed on the Compliance page. Access is restricted in jurisdictions where the service is not authorised.

— Q.04

Why is access gated?

Because we need to confirm who you are, where you live and whether you understand what you’re joining. The petition captures the minimum information required to evaluate your application.
