// The System

Built to pay members. Designed to be verified.

Most reward programmes advertise their best case and hide the maths. Most private clubs advertise access but not what members actually receive. Tycoon Club inverts both: the distribution record sits on a public chain, while the membership stays inside a private register. Three things make that work.

$

Members are paid in USDT

Distributions are not paid in a token whose price has to climb for the reward to be worth something. Members receive USDT — a dollar-stable instrument they can hold, swap, or withdraw.

⧉

Settlement is on Arbitrum

Every distribution settles on Arbitrum One, an Ethereum Layer 2. Fees are small, settlement is fast, and the public ledger inherits Ethereum-grade security.

✓

Every payout is on-chain

Every distribution the Club has made is a public, immutable transaction. Members verify the Club’s history themselves, using addresses provided in the Member Pack.

The Tycoon Club is not a fund. It does not pool member capital, hold deposits, or sell tokens. Distributions are payments to active members from the Club’s own operating activity.

// Two Engines

What a member earns is driven by two things.

Inside the Tycoon Club, rewards aren’t down to a single mechanic. A member’s standing is driven by how they play — and by the strength of the network they bring into the Club. Both are recorded; both settle on-chain.

Engine 01 · Play

The Arena

A member’s results in the Race to Earn arena — outcomes decided by play, recorded on-chain and settled in USDT on Arbitrum. The better the play, the stronger the standing.

Engine 02 · Network

Your Network

The strength of the network a member builds inside the Club. As the members they bring in take part in the arena, the member’s own standing in the Club grows alongside the network they’ve helped build.

// The Register

Your place is yours.

Trust isn’t a marketing claim. It’s the register: a private record, held by the Club, that says where each member sits and from when. The register is what makes belonging real.

// The Register · Sample Private · Members
#001 [name held] Admitted · Nov 2025
#247 [your place] Admitted · on enquiry
#412 [name held] Admitted · Feb 2026
#415 [name held] Admitted · Feb 2026

Every admitted member is entered into the register on the day they join. The register records the date of admission, the member’s number, and the standing they hold. It is private — only the Club and its members can see it.

A member’s place in the register is theirs from the moment they join. It is not auctioned. It is not re-issued. It cannot be bought ahead of an earlier date. Members rise through the recognition system from their fixed position over time.

This is what belonging means in the Club — a name, on a particular line, on a particular date, held by an organisation that distributes rewards to that register on a publicly verifiable ledger.

Public ledger. Private register. The two together are the Club.

// Verification

Verify before you join.

The Club’s distribution history is public on-chain. Members can verify what the Club has paid, when it was paid, and that it settled — before they ever join, and at any time afterwards.

For each distribution period, members receive a verification receipt that looks like this:

// Distribution ID   TC-2026-02-A
// Period           February 2026
// Network        Arbitrum One
// Settled to       415 distinct member addresses
// Aggregate        45,986 USDT
// Verifiable at   arbiscan.io/address/0x…   (sent in Member Pack)

The verification path is the same for everyone: look up the addresses on the Arbitrum explorer, read the transactions, confirm them against the figures the Club has published. For prospective members, the Club shares verification addresses on enquiry. For admitted members, the addresses are listed in the Member Pack and refreshed quarterly.
